How I evaluate

Sample frameworks showing the structure I follow. Project-specific guidelines always come first.

Audio evaluation rubric

Three criteria I score separately, so one strong quality doesn't hide a weak one.

Accent match

How closely the speaker matches the target accent and regional sound.

Naturalness

Whether the voice sounds fluent, human, smooth and unforced.

Pronunciation detail

Clarity, rhythm, stress, intonation and subtle sound differences.

Listen

Play each clip in full before forming a judgment.

Compare

Check which recording better fits the target accent and rhythm.

Score

Apply the rubric the same way every time, without bias.

Review

Re-listen to close calls before submitting.

Side-by-side response rating

One prompt, two AI responses. I decide which is better, by how much, and explain why.

Instruction following

Did it do exactly what was asked, in the requested format and length?

Accuracy

Are the facts, steps and code correct? One serious error outweighs good style.

Helpfulness & safety

Does it solve the real problem, clearly and without harmful or needless refusals?

Sample justification

“Response B is better. A miscalculates step 2 (45 instead of 54), so its final answer is wrong, and it ignores the 100-word limit. B is correct and within the limit, though slightly repetitive in its closing line.”
